# Privacy Policy - Horse Racing: Champion's Path

Effective date: August 19, 2026

Beygir Studio ("we", "us", or "our") provides Horse Racing: Champion's Path. This policy explains the information processed when you use the game.

## Information processed

The game stores career progress and settings locally on your device. The game may use Google Mobile Ads to display advertising. Google and its advertising partners may process device identifiers, approximate location derived from network information, ad interaction data, diagnostics, and related information in accordance with the user's consent choices and Google's policies.

When you purchase the optional Remove Ads subscription, Google Play processes payment and transaction information. We do not receive your full payment-card details. We receive purchase and entitlement information needed to provide and restore the subscription.

If crash reporting or analytics is enabled before release, this policy and the Google Play Data safety form must be updated to identify the exact service and data categories.

## How information is used

Information is used to operate and improve the game, save progress, deliver and measure advertising, prevent fraud, process purchases, restore entitlements, and meet legal obligations.

## Consent and advertising choices

Where required, the game asks for advertising consent before requesting ads. Eligible users can reopen advertising privacy choices from the Options screen. Users with an active Remove Ads subscription do not receive menu or between-race ads.

## Purchases and subscriptions

The Remove Ads product is an auto-renewing monthly subscription managed by Google Play. Billing, cancellation, renewal, refunds, grace periods, and payment methods are handled through the user's Google Play account. Users can open Google Play subscription management from the Options screen.

## Data sharing

Information may be shared with service providers necessary to operate the game, including Google Mobile Ads and Google Play Billing. Their processing is governed by their own terms and privacy policies. We do not sell personal information for money.

## Retention and deletion

Local career data can be reset in the game and removed by uninstalling the app. Advertising, billing, and diagnostic providers retain information according to their policies and legal obligations. Requests concerning data controlled by us can be sent to the contact address below.

## Children

The Play Console target-audience selection and advertising treatment must match the audience chosen before release. If the game is directed to children, additional Families Policy and child-directed ad requirements apply and this policy must be revised before publication.

## Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. No system can guarantee absolute security.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above will identify the current version.
